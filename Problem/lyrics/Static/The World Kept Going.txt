[INTRO — tight groove, intimate vocal]

Somewhere a window is glowing tonight,
Somebody's walking beneath the same light,
Somebody's name is still warm in the air,
Though nobody living remembers it there.

[VERSE I]

There was a room with a red-painted door,
Young hands were counting the minutes once more,
Smoke found the ceiling, the ceiling found flame,
Morning came asking for somebody's name.

A photograph waits in a drawer out of sight,
Faces still smiling like everything's right,
Years keep on running, the numbers grow old,
But some little stories were never retold.

[PRE-CHORUS]

And the city keeps moving,
The river keeps proving
That everything changes its shape in the rain,
But somewhere a heartbeat
Still walks through the street
Like memory refusing to fade.

[CHORUS — energetic, soaring]

So turn up the light, let it shine through the night,
Let it dance on the walls where they vanished from sight,
We carry the spark, we carry the scars,
We carry the names that were lost in the stars.

Turn up the light, let tomorrow arrive,
For the ones who went quiet before they could thrive,
If history sleeps, let the melody start,
Some stories disappear, but they stay in the heart.

[POST-CHORUS]

Oh-oh, keep the light alive,
Oh-oh, let the lost survive,
Not in the numbers, not in the stone,
But in every life that refuses to go cold.

[VERSE II]

There was a mind that could measure the sky,
A different kind of fire that they couldn't deny,
They asked it to bend, they asked it to hide,
And called something broken that burned from inside.

There was a girl with a page in her hand,
Writing a world that was harder to understand,
Outside were the footsteps, inside was a voice,
A little bit of hope making silence a choice.

Then somewhere the ground turned the night into day,
Walls lost their meaning, the streets lost their way,
A thousand small lives became one silent sound,
And morning found stories beneath the ground.

[PRE-CHORUS]

Still the world keeps moving,
The old clocks keep proving
That time doesn't stop for a name or a face,
But somewhere the echoes
Slip through the shadows
And quietly ask us to remember the place.

[BRIDGE — groove never stops, vocals become intimate]

No book can remember every place,

So when you see a door,
Think of what it kept.
When you hear a voice,
Think of what's left.

When the ground feels still,
When the room feels bare,
There may be someone
