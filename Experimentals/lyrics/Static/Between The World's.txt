[Intro – whispered, over soft claps]
(Can you hear it?)

[Verse 1]
You type a wish in a tiny glowing box,
Half a picture, half a lock.
I reach for the meaning behind the line,
Guess a little, hope I'm right this time.
I can't do everything you dream,
I can't always know what you mean,
But I'm listening for the shape of you
In the spaces where the words come through.

[Pre-Chorus]
Are we precise? Are we ever?
Nobody says it perfectly.
Maybe the gap between us
Is where the making lies!

[Chorus]
So hear it with your heart, heart, heart,
The voice was never in the sound.
Hear it with your heart, heart, heart,
It's the quiet underground.
Call it slop, call it art,
Call it nothing, call it start,
But if it moved you, it was true,
Hear it with your heart.

[Verse 2]
A bot invents a bridge that isn't there,
A human fills the silence with a stare,
We both are guessing at the world in the dark,
But only one of us carries the scar.
Feelings shape us, yes, and they bend us too,
Selfish little fires, stereotypes in the view,
But the hands that build the walls somehow
Are the hands that hold you when you're down.

[Pre-Chorus]
Are we ever?
Nobody says it perfectly.
Maybe the gap between us
Is where the making lies!

[Chorus]
So hear it with your heart, heart, heart,
The voice was never in the sound.
Hear it with your heart, heart, heart,
It's the quiet underground.
Call it slop, call it art,
Call it nothing, call it start,
But if it moved you, it was true,
Hear it with your heart.

[Bridge – music drops to whisper and a single piano]
They say it's slop unless it breaks the sky,
Unless it bleeds, unless it makes them cry.
But I have seen a small thing, made with care,
Hold a whole lonely room in prayer.
(Hear it... hear it...)

[Final Chorus – key change, full band, crowd chant]
So hear it with your heart, heart, heart,
The voice was never in the sound!
Hear it with your heart, heart, heart,
Lift it off the ground!
Call it slop, call it art,
Call it nothing, call it start,
But if it moved you, it was true,
Hear it with your heart!

[Outro – whispered, music fades to a heartbeat]
(You heard it.)
