[INTRO — CLEAN PIANO, ONE SMALL MOTIF, ROOM TONE]

I remember the morning,
The light across the floor,
A blue sky through the window,
And nothing more.

[VERSE 1 — WARM, ORGANIC, STEADY]

I remember coffee cooling,
Someone laughing down the hall,
I remember summer breathing
Through the curtains on the wall.

There was music from the kitchen,
There was sunlight in the street,
I remember someone walking
Barefoot beside me.

[SMALL INTERRUPTION — WHISPERED]

It rained that morning.

[PAUSE]

No...
I remember the sun.

[VERSE 2 — SAME MELODY, BUT ONE NOTE IS NOW WRONG]

I remember rain against the window,
Silver running down the glass,
I remember staying inside
And watching hours slowly pass.

There was a song beneath the thunder,
Something soft I can't recall,
I remember you were singing...

[WHISPER]

There wasn't any music.

[INSTRUMENTAL — THE ORIGINAL MOTIF RETURNS, ALTERED]

Was the sky blue?
Was the sky grey?
Did I wake up?
Did I dream the day?

[CHORUS — EXPLOSIVE, EMOTIONAL POP-ROCK]

I remember the wrong day,
I remember it so clear,
I can see the shape of everything,
I just can't make it real.

I remember your voice,
I remember your face,
But every time I reach for you,
The memory changes place.

Was I there?
Were you there?
Did it happen?
Did I care?

I remember the wrong day,
And I don't know what is true.

[VERSE 3 — STYLE SHIFTS, MORE RHYTHMIC, MELODY FRAGMENTED]

I remember yellow walls,
No, they were white.
I remember leaving
Before midnight.

I remember your jacket,
I remember the door,
I remember saying,
“I've been here before.”

But the room was never yellow.
There was never any door.
And the person I remember
Had never met me before.

[BEAT DROPS OUT]

So who was I remembering?

[BRIDGE — BARE PIANO, CLOSE VOCAL]

What color was the sky?

I don't know.

What song was playing?

I don't know.

What did the room smell like?

I don't know.

What did you say to me?

...

I don't know.

[PIANO MOTIF — SLOWLY LOSING NOTES]

I don't remember the day.

I remember remembering it.

[BUILD — OLD MELODIES RETURN IN WRONG FORMS]

Maybe the rain was sunlight,
Maybe silence sounded like a song,
Maybe you were never with me,
Maybe I've been wrong all along.

Maybe every time I told it,
I changed another piece,
Until the story became something
That was easier to keep.

[FINAL CHORUS — MASSIVE, MULTIPLE “MEMORIES” COLLIDING]

I remember the wrong day,
I remember every lie,
Every color, every shadow,
Every version in my mind.

I remember your voice,
Though I don't know if it was you,
I remember what I lost,
Though I don't know if I ever knew.

Was I there?
Were you there?
Was it real?
Was it fear?

I remember the wrong day,
And the day remembers me.

[BREAKDOWN — MUSIC COLLAPSING]

I remember the morning...

No.

I remember the rain...

No.

There was music...

No.

You were there...

No.

[NEAR SILENCE]

Maybe it never happened that way.

[LONG PAUSE]

Maybe that's how I remember it.

[FINAL PIANO — THE ORIGINAL MOTIF RETURNS, BUT THE LAST NOTE IS WRONG]

I remember...

[STOP]
